How to Choose a Roofing Contractor in Houston: Five Checks Before You Sign

Texas is one of the few states that does not require a state roofing licence — Epic Roofing TX makes this point plainly on its homepage: anyone with a ladder and a magnetic sign can market themselves as a roofer. That makes the contractor check more important than the quote. Work through these five checks before you sign anything.

1. A real local address

Ask where the company is actually based, and whether it will still be there in a year. Epic Roofing TX is headquartered at 9402 Spaulding St, Houston, TX 77016, and serves Houston, Katy, Sugar Land, Cypress, The Woodlands, Pearland, League City, Spring, Pasadena and Tomball. Out-of-state plates and a PO box are a warning sign.

2. A written, itemised estimate — before work starts

Get the scope in writing: materials, tiers, warranty options and the full price, with no hidden escalations. Epic Roofing TX offers free inspections with upfront written estimates and no obligation. If a contractor will only quote verbally, walk away.

3. Warranty terms in writing

Ask exactly what the workmanship warranty covers, for how long, and whether it is transferable. Epic Roofing TX includes a written 10-year workmanship guarantee on full replacements, covering flashings, valleys, ridge caps and pipe boots against leaks. Verbal promises mean nothing — get the document.

4. Insurance-claim help done properly

A good Houston roofer documents damage with date-stamped high-resolution photos, produces an itemised estimate, and meets your insurance adjuster on the roof. One legal line matters: under Texas law, a contractor may not pay, waive or absorb your insurance deductible. Anyone promising a "free roof" by absorbing the deductible is asking you to break the law.

5. No pressure, no same-day contract push

High-pressure same-day contract tactics are a red flag. A reputable contractor gives you the photos, the written estimate and time to decide — even if the honest answer is a small repair rather than a big replacement.
